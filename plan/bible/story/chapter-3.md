# Bible · story · Chapter 3: Bayhaven Tomorrow

The outline of the third chapter: the arc, seven new characters and twenty missions, numbered 41 to 60. Two years on, Pelican Rest owns itself, Mags chairs the association she never wanted, and the Reef is being dredged to float a city for the very rich. It ends on The Rig in a storm, with all twelve Regulars alongside. Every mission runs on the systems in `04-systems.md` plus six small new ones, named below.

Tier: D, all of it.

## The arc

Two years after the launch at Cape Calamity. Pelican Rest has a new sign, "A self-owned community", a picnic table for meetings and a rulebook that is already nine pages long. Mags is president of the Pelican Rest Homeowners' Association. She runs meetings in eight minutes with a gavel made from a gear knob, and she has caught herself measuring a lawn. Teo is 26. His two million followers are 900,000 and falling, because a 16-year-old called Wren Okafor is funnier than he is.

Out past the Keys a dredger called the Lamprey is eating the Reef. In March it ran down a shrimp boat at night and did not stop. One man drowned. The sand goes to **Above It All**, a floating city sold to the very rich as a place weather cannot reach. Its founder, **Magnus Wrasse**, gives a keynote every hour. Its money is Constance Vane's. Its Resident Security carries rifles, and the first engineer to object went over the side on a calm night. Its Director of Resident Happiness is Gordon Bellweather, in a lanyard, still smiling.

**Act 1 · Madam President (41 to 45).** Life as the thing they fought: motions, quorums, a neighbour who objects. Then buoys go missing on the Reef, sandbars move, and a white module the size of a hotel is moored in front of the park's sunset.

**Act 2 · Above It All (46 to 50).** They go aboard. They tow a building. They take an engineer who knows the modules will not survive a storm, a lighthouse keeper with forty years of logbooks, and, with Wren, they put the Reef itself on camera.

**Act 3 · Small Craft Advisory (51 to 55).** Dale Rucker is back, on the water, with Wrasse as sponsor. Madame Calypso says a storm with a name is nine days out. Nilda raises a fleet. The association votes to spend everything it has, and Bellweather turns up at the picnic table without his smile.

**Act 4 · The Rig (56 to 60).** Wrasse chains his modules to The Rig, the one fixed thing on the Reef, with four hundred members aboard. The storm arrives. So does everybody else.

**How it ends.** The modules are cut loose and towed into shelter. Four hundred members step off alive, which was not the forecast. Not all of Wrasse's security do. The Lamprey is stopped by a steel cube. Above It All relocates to "calmer jurisdictions" and the county makes the Reef a park. At the last meeting Mags moves to delete the rulebook, all but one line.

**What changes in the leads.** Mags finds out that power is only a bus: it matters who is driving and whether they brake. Teo makes something that is not about him, and it is the best thing he has made.
