# Bible · radio · Ads

The shared pool of 48 radio ads, every brand from the canon, some of them selling to people who have just shot someone. Ads 1 to 6 are the six samples from `05-content.md`, kept word for word; they are short on purpose and double as the only ads Low Tide Lounge will run. Ads 7 to 48 are 25 to 45 words each. Each entry gives the tier, the voice, the stations that run it and the script as the ticker shows it.

Tier: A for ads 1 to 24 (the pool of 24 the roadmap asks for on day 8). B for 25 to 37. C for 38 to 44. D for 45 to 48, which sell places in Marlin County.

## How the pool works

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| When | One ad per station loop, after the host line and before the ident |
| Pick | At random from the ads that list the station, never repeating until all of them have played there |
| Not built yet | An ad whose station has not shipped simply never plays there. An ad of a later tier is skipped until its tier ships. |
| Low Tide Lounge | Runs ads 1 to 6 only, whispered, one every second loop |
| Bayhaven Public Radio | Barbara reads every ad herself and opens it with "Support for this station comes from" and the brand name |
| The Grind 24/7 | Every third ad slot is one of Kiki's own sponsored segments instead |
| Day rules | Where an entry names a day, the ad plays three times as often on that day and never on the others if it says "only" |
| Storm rule | Ad 4 plays first in any loop that starts during rain, a tropical storm or a hurricane |
| Sting | Two marimba notes before an ad (fifth, then root of the station's current key) and one after (root) |
| Length | An ad scrolls once on the ticker at 14 characters a second. Music is ducked 6 dB under it. |
| Language | Ads may swear. Barbara never does: on Bayhaven Public Radio she reads any swear word as "a word I won't read". |

## Voices

| Voice | Speech blip |
|---|---|
| announcer | Square through a 2 kHz low-pass, 250 Hz, 45 ms per syllable, 9 syllables a second. The brand name is said 10% higher. |
| Larry | 200 Hz, fast, every sentence ends on a rise. Use the cast sheet's pitch if it sets one. |
| Annie | 290 Hz, slower than Larry, every sentence ends on a fall |
| Kiki | Canon: 340 Hz with vibrato |
| the Mayor | 310 Hz, with a 100 ms pause for applause after her own name |
| the Sheriff | Canon: 120 Hz with radio crackle. His swearing is written `(bleep)` and sounded as a 1 kHz tone for 250 ms. |
| captain | The announcer's blip slowed to 6 syllables a second through a 300 Hz to 3 kHz band-pass, with tape flutter. A very old recording. |
