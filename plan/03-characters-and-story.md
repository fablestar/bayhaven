# 03 · Characters and story

Everything here is original. No character, name, plot beat or line is taken from another game, film or show, and none is based on a real person.

## Tone

A sunny crime comedy. Think of a postcard with something going wrong in the corner of it.

**What the jokes are about:** homeowner associations with too much power · wellness influencers · luxury condo developers · a sheriff who polices for TV ratings · billboard lawyers · "Local Man" news stories · the weather, always the weather · everyone filming everything.

**What the jokes are never about:** real people, real companies, or any group of people for who they are. Nobody is the butt of a joke for being old, poor, foreign or different. The targets are people with power who abuse it.

**Violence** is real inside the fiction, as the genre expects: people get shot, cars blow up, crews and deputies shoot back, and what the leads do has consequences. It is drawn low-poly and played with dark humour, never lingered on. Characters swear when they would. Out: slurs, jokes aimed at any group for who they are, explicit sexual content, sexual violence, harm to children, real people.

## The leads

You play both and can switch between them from day 7.

### Mags Calloway, 61
- **Was:** the best getaway driver on the coast, in her twenties. Stock-car racing at the Speedbowl was the cover; she won there in a car she built. Eleven jobs, never caught, never charged, never said a name. Then she walked away and drove a school bus for 31 years, the cleanest cover there is. Never had an accident. Never raised her voice, because she never needed to.
- **Now:** retired at Pelican Rest, a trailer park behind the beach, where she fixes everybody's cars, knows who to call when a car needs to stop existing, and feeds a pelican she calls Gerald.
- **Wants:** to keep the home she paid for. She knows exactly how far she will go for it, and it scares her a little that she misses it.
- **Looks like:** silver hair in a short ponytail, driving gloves, a floral shirt, cargo shorts, a visor. Small and upright.
- **Plays like:** the driver. Every vehicle grips 15% better and takes 25% less damage with her at the wheel. Slower on foot (sprint 5.0 m/s), steadier aim.
- **Ability, Bus Driver Voice:** a blast on the horn that makes traffic pull over and people freeze for 5 seconds. 30-second cooldown. Works on police cars at heat 1 and 2 only.
- **Speech blip:** low and dry, 180 Hz.

### Teo Navarro, 24
- **Was:** Mags's neighbour's grandson. Came to stay for a summer four years ago, after a hustle up the coast went bad, and never left.
- **Now:** a small-time crook with a stream. 212 followers, most of them bots, one of them Mags. Resells phones that fell off a truck, runs packages for Rafa on a delivery scooter, and films everything, including things he should not.
- **Wants:** to go viral, once, for anything, and to be taken seriously by people who do this for real.
- **Looks like:** bleached hair, an open floral shirt over a tank top, slides with socks, a phone on a chest mount.
- **Plays like:** the runner. Fastest on foot (sprint 6.8 m/s), climbs walls up to 3 m, lighter in fights.
- **Ability, Go Live:** starts a stream for 20 seconds. Followers earned are doubled and nearby collectibles show on the minimap, but heat rises 50% faster. 60-second cooldown. He can also fly a small camera drone to scout (bonus pool).
- **Speech blip:** quick and bright, 260 Hz.

**Why they work together:** she is a retired professional who wants no attention. He is an amateur who wants all of it. She can drive anything; he can get in anywhere. Every job they pull is pulled between those two things, and the city's real crooks take them more seriously with every one.

## The story: "Notice to Vacate"

Twenty missions in four acts. Seven of them (marked **A**) form a complete short story with a beginning, a middle and the ending, and those are what the day 9 build ships at the middle compute level. **B** missions are added next, **C** last. Full step lists for the A missions are in `05-content.md`.

## Cutscenes

A puppet show, not a film.

- Camera keyframes, characters walking to marks, subtitles with a small portrait, a speech blip per line.
- 20–45 seconds each. One tap skips. Nothing important is only in a cutscene.
- Four ship with the A missions: the opening (the notice goes up, Gerald watches), the switch introduction before mission 11, landfall (mission 18 opens on it) and the ending (night and morning shots).
- Dialogue is short. No line is longer than 90 characters, so it fits a phone in one row or two.

Sample, mission 1:

> **Teo:** They took my scooter. My whole career is on that scooter.
> **Mags:** Your career is a phone on a stick.
> **Teo:** Are you going to help?
> **Mags:** *(pulling the cover off the coupe)* Seatbelt.

## The world talks back

Three cheap systems make the city feel like it noticed you. Details in `04-systems.md` and `05-content.md`.

- **Squawk**, the in-game social app. Posts react to what you just did ("someone is doing donuts outside Hotel Alba and honestly? needed").
- **Bayhaven Public Radio.** The news reads back your last chase as a "Local Man" story.
- **Followers.** Teo's follower count is a second score. People on the street start to recognise you past 10,000.

## The Regulars (tiers B and C)

Twelve side characters, each with a short chain of jobs: 46 jobs in all. Three of them (Lawyer Larry, "Zoom" Ziegler and Gus Thibodeaux) are in the bonus pool; the other nine come after release. The roster is in `bible/00-canon.md`; every job is scripted in `bible/story/regulars-1.md` and `regulars-2.md`.

## After chapter 1 (full vision, tier D)

"Notice to Vacate" is chapter 1 of three. Nothing below is built before release.

- **Chapter 2 · Hurricane Season.** The storm is over and the bill arrives. Bellweather is gone, but the Seawall Group that lent him the money now owns half the damaged city. The Sheriff, his show cancelled, runs for mayor. Teo has two million followers and no idea what to do with them. Twenty missions about rebuilding the town before someone else buys it.
- **Chapter 3 · Bayhaven Tomorrow.** Two years on, Pelican Rest owns itself and Mags is, to her horror, president of a homeowners' association. The Reef is being dredged for a floating city for the very rich. Twenty missions that end on The Rig in a storm with every Regular turning up to help.

Outlines for all forty missions are in `bible/story/chapter-2.md` and `chapter-3.md`. Full scripts for chapter 1, line by line, are in `bible/story/act-1.md` to `act-4.md`.
